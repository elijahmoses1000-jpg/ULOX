=============================================================================
THE CORE SIX — start here
=============================================================================

--- 01-what-is-ulox.mp4 -----------------------------------------------------
Getting a model to answer is the easy part. Getting a system to keep working is
the job nobody teaches. UloX hands you 18 broken agent systems and asks you to
repair them — prompt, context, harness, loop, graph, agentic, in that order.

--- 02-fix-the-break.mp4 ----------------------------------------------------
Every drill in UloX starts already broken. An agent that never stops. A router
with two branches that both match. A guard that logs and lets it through. You
get a limited number of moves, and a beam search is looking for the repair too
— so the hint is a real line of play, not a nudge.

--- 03-six-disciplines.mp4 --------------------------------------------------
"Prompt engineering" is one sixth of the job. The other five: context, harness,
loop, graph, agentic. UloX teaches them in that order because each one is
useless without the last — a loop you cannot stop is usually a context problem
wearing a different hat.

--- 04-play-someone.mp4 -----------------------------------------------------
You can play someone. Rated matches on UloX deal both sides the identical five
broken systems, so nobody gets the easier board — and your Glicko-2 rating
moves on the result. There is a live map of who is online, opt-in, rounded to
about 11km on your own device before it is ever sent.

--- 05-certificates.mp4 -----------------------------------------------------
Finish a discipline on UloX — read it, fix every drill, build the thing — and
you get a certificate with a code anyone can check on a public page. Download
it as a PDF for printing or a JPG for posting. Both carry the verification
link, so the proof travels with the image.

--- 06-the-library.mp4 ------------------------------------------------------
250 terms in plain words. 154 one-page field notes. 94 learning roadmaps with
their real wiring, where every node opens its own topic and its videos. UloX
fetches and parses all of it from source, so you stop hunting through tabs.

=============================================================================
THE MECHANISMS — how it actually works
=============================================================================

--- 07-itols.mp4 ------------------------------------------------------------
Most prompts are prose with hope in them. UloX makes you write a contract
instead: Identity, Tasks, Outcome, Language, Security. Five fields you can
diff, test and grade — because a prompt you cannot test is not engineering.

--- 08-the-eval-bar.mp4 -----------------------------------------------------
Chess players have known for decades that every position has a score. UloX puts
the same bar on your agent: one number, -9.9 to +9.9, recomputed on every move
by a simulator rather than an opinion. You stop guessing whether your fix made
it better.

--- 09-install-it.mp4 -------------------------------------------------------
No download, no app store. UloX installs straight from the browser onto your
phone, tablet or desktop, and still opens when the signal goes. Sign in and
your rating, streak and progress follow you to any device.

--- 10-jargon-desk.mp4 ------------------------------------------------------
You hit a word you do not know, and six tabs later you have lost the thread.
UloX has a jargon desk built in: 250 terms in plain words — what it means, why
it matters, what people confuse it with. Type "RAG", get Retrieval-Augmented
Generation. The short forms are indexed too.

--- 11-roadmaps.mp4 ---------------------------------------------------------
94 learning roadmaps, and every node is live — tap one for its explanation, its
resources and its videos without leaving the map. Fetched from source along
with the edge geometry, which is why the paths actually connect.

--- 12-three-levels.mp4 -----------------------------------------------------
Most tools are written for the people who built them. UloX asks where you are —
beginner, intermediate or advanced — and changes the wording, not just the
colour. Same drills, explained the way you need them explained. Switch any
time; nothing resets.

--- 13-the-lab.mp4 ----------------------------------------------------------
Once you can fix a broken agent, build one from nothing. Drag the nodes, wire
the edges, run it — graded by the same simulator that grades the drills. Every
discipline ends with a build, which is what the certificate actually means.

--- 14-puzzle-rush.mp4 ------------------------------------------------------
Three minutes. How many broken agent systems can you fix in a row? One wrong
move ends the run. Recognition is the real skill — seeing "this is a retry
storm" in two seconds is what separates reading about it from knowing it.

--- 15-the-hint.mp4 ---------------------------------------------------------
Most hints are a sentence someone typed in advance. UloX searches the position
and hands you the first move of a solution it actually found — the way a chess
engine shows a line. It cannot point you somewhere that does not work.

--- 16-rating-and-streak.mp4 ------------------------------------------------
"I think I'm getting better" is not good enough. Chess solved this in 1960.
UloX gives you a Glicko-2 rating that moves on what you actually solved, with a
confidence interval so a lucky run does not inflate it.

=============================================================================
THE DEPTH — what is underneath
=============================================================================

--- 17-make-it-safe.mp4 -----------------------------------------------------
Your agent reads a poisoned document. Your guard noticed and let it through
anyway — guard theatre is the most common agent vulnerability there is. UloX
drills prompt injection, excessive agency, unbounded delegation and budget
blindness until bounded, observable and fail-safe are habits.

--- 18-field-notes.mp4 ------------------------------------------------------
You know the idea; you forgot the detail. 154 one-page field notes on agent
harnesses, state machines, MCP, A2A, benchmarks, evals and retrieval —
eighteen of them wired directly into the six stages, so the reference appears
at the moment you need it.

--- 19-open-libraries.mp4 ---------------------------------------------------
Everyone bookmarks the big repos. Nobody opens them again, because a
400,000-word README is not a tool. UloX parses eight of them — public APIs,
build-your-own-x, free programming books, awesome, freeCodeCamp — into
something searchable, and falls back to upstream if a fork goes quiet.

--- 20-one-next-step.mp4 ----------------------------------------------------
People do not quit learning because it is hard. They quit because forty tabs
are open and none of them is obviously next. UloX shows you one thing: read
this, or fix this, or build this. Finish it and the next one appears.

--- 21-who-its-for.mp4 ------------------------------------------------------
Everyone can call a model now, which is exactly the problem — almost nobody can
make one reliable. UloX is for engineers shipping their first agent, teams
whose agent works on Tuesdays, and anyone who was handed "add AI to it". Free,
open, and in your browser.
